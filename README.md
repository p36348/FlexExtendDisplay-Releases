# Flex Extend Display

Turn your iPad into a smooth, responsive extended display for your Mac.
Add more room for your timeline, documents, messages, reference material, and
everyday multitasking—without adding another monitor to your desk or travel bag.

[Download Flex Extend Display for macOS](https://github.com/p36348/FlexExtendDisplay-Releases/releases/latest)
·
[Get the iPad app on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6752341387)

## Made for a more flexible workspace

- **Up to 120 Hz:** Enjoy fluid motion on supported iPad models.
- **Touch control:** Interact with your Mac from the streamed screen on iPad.
- **Wireless or USB:** Work cable-free or connect by USB for a more consistent
  connection.
- **Flexible orientation:** Use your iPad in landscape or portrait to suit your
  workspace.
- **Local connection:** Display data stays between your Mac and iPad rather than
  being streamed through a remote cloud service.

## Get started

1. Install the [iPad app from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6752341387).
2. Download the latest macOS DMG from the [Releases page](https://github.com/p36348/FlexExtendDisplay-Releases/releases/latest).
3. Open the DMG and drag **Flex Extend Display** to your Applications folder.
4. Open Flex Extend Display on both devices.
5. Connect the devices by USB, or make sure they are on the same local network.
6. Select your iPad in the Mac app to start using it as an extended display.

To control your Mac by touching the iPad screen, grant the optional
**Accessibility** permission when prompted by the Mac app.

## Requirements

- A Mac running **macOS 14.0 or later**
- An iPad running **iPadOS 18.0 or later**
- The Flex Extend Display app installed on both devices

Full features—including 120 Hz display, rotation, and Mac extended-display
functionality—require an in-app subscription in the iPad app. Feature
availability may depend on the capabilities of your Mac and iPad.

## Safe downloads and automatic updates

The macOS app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
After installation, the app can check for updates automatically and verifies
each update before installing it.
